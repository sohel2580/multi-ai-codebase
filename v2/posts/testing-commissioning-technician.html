Testing and Commissioning Technician Responsibilities Explained

2026-08-01 4 min read Sohel Ahammad
#Testing and Commissioning #Electrical Work #Saudi Arabia

Testing and commissioning (T&C) work is one of the most vital stages in any electrical or industrial project. It involves verifying that equipment, systems, and connections are operating safely and correctly according to design specifications before a project reaches full operational service.

The Importance of Precision in T&C

During testing at the Jubail 3B IWP, we verified electrical panels, switches, and SCADA communication lines. It is not only about technical knowledge but also about methodical execution, documentation, and safe working habits.

  • Adjusted and operated tested electrical materials and instrumentation equipment.
  • Tested SCADA levels of communication for accurate telemetry and remote control.
  • Documented test results, phase alignment, and continuity logs for engineering approval.
  • Troubleshot communication gaps between field devices and the main control room.

Professional Growth

This experience helped me develop confidence in handling equipment checks, understanding complex electrical architectures, and supporting overall project quality and safety standards in Saudi Arabia.
