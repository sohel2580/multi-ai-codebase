Store Keeper Skills: Inventory, Stock Checks, and Records

2026-08-01 3 min read Sohel Ahammad
#Store Keeping #Inventory Management #Office Support

Store keeping may look simple from the outside, but it demands strong organization, consistency, and responsibility. Managing inventory accurately helps prevent delays, reduces waste, and supports smooth operations across project sites.

Core Duties & Inventory Systems

Working with Shapoorji Pallonji in King Abdullah Economic City, I was responsible for receiving, sorting, and issuing materials for active construction zones.

  • Received, inspected, sorted, and securely stored materials in warehouse stockrooms.
  • Conducted periodic physical stock checks to verify digital inventory accuracy.
  • Managed material requisition forms, delivery notes, and sales receipts.
  • Maintained clear accounting and stock log records for audits.

Transferable Skills

These responsibilities built my ability to be dependable, meticulous with numerical records, and comfortable handling administrative duties that keep operations running smoothly.
