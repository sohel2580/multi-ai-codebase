My Work Experience as an Electrical Terminator in Saudi Arabia

2026-08-01 3 min read Sohel Ahammad
#Saudi Arabia #Electrical Termination #Industrial Work

I started building my professional experience in Saudi Arabia by taking on practical industrial roles where discipline, safety, and accuracy mattered every day. As an electrical terminator, I worked closely with cable systems, wiring, inspection, and testing tasks that required patience and strong attention to detail.

Key Responsibilities & Challenges

In major industrial and plant projects—such as the Jubail 3B Independent Water Project and Aramco oil plant operations—cable termination requires precise stripping, crimping, glanding, and connection according to exact schematics and safety standards.

  • Connected and disconnected heavy-duty cables, wires, and conductors.
  • Inspected and tested terminations for accuracy, continuity, and insulation resistance.
  • Identified faulty and burnt wiring resulting from operational stress and performed safe, certified replacements.
  • Strictly adhered to Aramco and international site safety protocols.

Key Takeaways

My experience taught me how to stay focused during demanding work conditions, follow instructions carefully, and contribute to project goals without compromising quality. For employers looking for a dependable technician, this experience reflects a strong foundation in electrical work and the ability to adapt quickly to industrial environments.
